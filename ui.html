✂ Clip to UH Homes

Browser extension

Reads the page from inside your browser, so it works even on sites that block the app itself. It scans the whole page for product details — price, brand, model #, finish, dimensions, materials — wherever the vendor put them, lets you edit every detail before saving, and can send the clip straight onto a project's spec sheet as well as into your library.

⬇ Download the clipper

1. Unzip the download. 2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. 3. Click the extension's icon and paste this app's address and a clipper token.

The token identifies you to this workspace and expires after 90 days. Treat it like a password.
